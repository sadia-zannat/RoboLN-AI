# Procedural assets and licenses

All supplied 3D geometry is project-authored in `frontend/components/procedural-parts.tsx`; component assembly definitions are in `frontend/lib/component-library.ts`. No external GLB, texture, HDR or manufacturer CAD is required. The locally generated studio cube environment uses area-light geometry. Renderer packages are installed from the exact npm lockfile and retain their own licenses.

Included generic families: chassis/deck/rails/fasteners; treaded wheels with hubs/bolts; motors/gearboxes; controller PCB/chips/heatsink/connectors/pins; camera/lens/mast; moisture probe; distance transducers; vented environmental sensor; enclosed power module; pump and mounting bracket. Appearance proves no protection rating, voltage, exact dimensions, calibration or mechanical performance.

Everything is PROCEDURAL_PROVISIONAL. World-space positions and Euler rotations are engineering drafts. Parent IDs are logical hierarchy, not articulated CAD constraints. No verified manufacturer assets or physical scale are claimed. No third-party binary model is bundled. No new license is imposed on the user-owned RoboLN project. Three.js, R3F, drei and three-stdlib are MIT-licensed dependencies; complete notices remain in their installed distributions.
